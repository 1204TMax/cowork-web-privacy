Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Co-work processes, which third-party services we work with, how AI data sharing consent and content reporting work, and how you can manage or delete your data.

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

Summary: Co-work uses an anonymous device-based account model. Before sending your first AI request, Co-work asks for your explicit permission to share your submitted prompts, files, images, and necessary context with third-party AI relay services and model providers. You can review or revoke this permission at any time in the app. We do not sell your personal information or track you across third-party apps or websites.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Co-work applications on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS (including App Store and direct macOS editions) and the related backend services used to operate AI chat, web search, webpage reading and document parsing, Mac Agent tasks, assistants, image generation, content reporting, feedback, credits, and Apple auto-renewable subscriptions.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, we may process the following categories of data:

  • Anonymous account and device identifiers: a locally generated device identifier, the internal anonymous user ID linked to it, and authentication tokens.
  • Account, membership, and credits state: membership tier, credit balances, usage counters, and billing ledger records.
  • Chat, search, and document content: prompts, conversation titles, message history, selected model, assistant context, URLs you ask AI to read, attached images or documents (such as PDF, Word, or PowerPoint files), and AI responses returned to you.
  • Voice input (iOS): when you use microphone dictation on iOS, audio is processed through Apple's system Speech Recognition framework to transcribe speech into text before you choose to send it.
  • Mac Agent task content: task instructions you submit, selected workspace, permission preferences, tool or command outputs, local file paths, metadata, and relevant file content only when you select or authorize the Agent to inspect, summarize, edit, or create files. Local workspace files remain on your Mac unless sent as part of an authorized task.
  • Image generation data: prompts, optional reference images, size, resolution, quality parameters, and generated outputs.
  • Content reports and feedback: report target metadata, selected report reason, optional details, optional excerpt of the reported AI output, the generated ticket ID (CR-...), and feedback messages with any optional email address you provide.
  • Local app settings: language preference, selected model, AI data sharing preference, workspace settings, and permission choices stored on your device.
  • Subscription and purchase verification records: Apple product identifiers, transaction IDs, original transaction IDs, signed transaction information or receipts, environment, expiration data, and the app account token used to associate a purchase with your anonymous account. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Push notification tokens (iOS): in iOS release builds, if you enable notifications, we process your Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token to notify you when background replies finish.
  • Product analytics and security data: privacy-safe product interaction events, membership status, app diagnostics, IP address, and request metadata used for rate limiting, request signing, fraud prevention, and service reliability. Chat prompts, AI replies, and user file contents are never sent to product analytics tools.

3. How We Use Data

We process data strictly to operate, secure, and improve Co-work, including to:

  • Create and maintain your anonymous account, membership entitlements, and credit balance.
  • Deliver AI chat, grounded web search, webpage reading, document parsing, assistant, Mac Agent, and image-generation features after you grant AI data sharing permission.
  • Store and sync conversation history and generated results for your anonymous account.
  • Process AI content reports, immediately remove reported outputs from your history, and exclude reported replies from subsequent AI model context.
  • Verify purchases, restore subscriptions, handle renewals, and maintain accounting and anti-fraud records.
  • Send reply-completion push notifications on iOS when enabled.
  • Measure product performance, diagnose crashes or errors, prevent abuse, and respond to support or deletion requests.

4. Subscription and Payment Information

Subscription payments are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. We do not receive your full payment card details. When you purchase, renew, upgrade, or restore a subscription, we process the transaction records Apple provides to verify the purchase, apply membership benefits, and maintain accounting, anti-fraud, and support records.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We share data with third-party service providers only to the extent necessary to deliver the specific features you use:

5.1 AI Relay Services and Model Providers

Only after you explicitly allow AI data sharing in the app, the prompts, necessary conversation context, selected images or files, URLs, and task parameters you submit pass through Co-work's backend and may be sent to the following third-party services depending on the model or feature you choose:

  • API Relay Services (3):
    • ZenMux (zenmux.ai): routes chat, grounded web search, and Mac Agent requests for supported models.
    • OpenRouter (openrouter.ai): routes supported chat and grounded web search requests according to service configuration and region.
    • Kie.ai (NEXUSAI SERVICES LLC) (kie.ai): processes AI image generation (such as GPT Image 2) and uploaded reference images.
  • AI Model Providers (8):
    • Via relay services: OpenAI (GPT chat and image models), Anthropic (Claude chat and Agent models), and Google (Gemini models and grounded web search).
    • Direct backend integrations: DeepSeek (chat, Mac Agent, webpage fetching, and document OCR/parsing), Zhipu AI (GLM models), Moonshot AI (Kimi models), MiniMax (MiniMax models), and Xiaomi (MiMo models).

These providers receive your request data solely to generate your requested responses, search summaries, parsed document content, images, or Agent outputs. We review provider data-protection terms and do not authorize them to use your data for cross-company advertising. Please avoid submitting highly sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your task.

5.2 Apple Services

  • App Store & StoreKit: for In-App Purchase processing, subscription verification, restoration, and subscription status notifications.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs): for delivering background reply notifications on iOS release builds when you grant notification permission.
  • Apple Speech Recognition: for transcribing voice input to text on iOS when you use microphone dictation.

5.3 Analytics Services

  • Google Firebase Analytics (iOS release builds): used in iOS App Store builds to record app usage events (such as screen views, feature interactions, and membership state) and measure stability. Prompts, chat messages, AI outputs, and file contents are never sent to Firebase Analytics.
  • Co-work First-Party Analytics: privacy-safe product interaction events and error diagnostics sent to Co-work's own backend (excluding prompts, chat text, and file contents). On macOS, optional product analytics is off by default for new users and can be controlled in Settings.

5.4 Cloud Infrastructure & Legal Requirements

  • Cloudflare: hosts Co-work's backend services (Workers), operational databases (D1), attachment and image storage (R2), and edge security/routing.
  • Legal or safety compliance: we may disclose limited information when required by applicable law, lawful process, or to protect user and service safety.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use collected data for cross-app advertising tracking.

6. AI Data Sharing Controls & Content Reporting

  • Explicit AI consent and revocation: before your first AI request, Co-work displays a consent prompt explaining what data is sent and which third-party AI providers may receive it. You can decline without losing access to app settings or subscription management, and you can review or revoke permission at any time in iOS (Profile → Data Sharing) or macOS (Settings → Privacy). When turned off, no new prompts, images, or files are sent to AI providers until you allow again.
  • Reporting and removing AI outputs: every AI text reply, Agent reply, and generated image provides a Report option. Once submitted, the reported item is immediately replaced in your view with a removal notice and ticket ID (CR-...), remains removed across refreshes and restarts, and is excluded from future AI model context. On macOS, reporting an Agent reply never modifies or deletes files in your local workspace.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain subscription records, handle content reports, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations:

  • Conversation history, generated image records, and Agent task records are retained until you delete them in the app or request account deletion.
  • Content report records and feedback submissions are retained for safety review, abuse prevention, and support tracking.
  • Subscription verification, billing, and anti-fraud records may be retained for accounting and compliance purposes.
  • If you delete an anonymous account that still has an active Apple subscription, we retain only irreversible HMAC fingerprints of the original transaction ID and app account token so you can restore valid entitlements to a new anonymous account. These fingerprints contain no raw transaction IDs, chats, or files and are marked used after restoration.
  • Security and rate-limiting metadata is retained for a limited operational period.

8. Your Choices and Deletion Requests

  • You can enable or turn off third-party AI data sharing at any time in the app settings.
  • You can report and remove any AI-generated message or image directly in the app.
  • You can delete individual conversations in the app, and on macOS you can also initiate anonymous account deletion in Settings → Account.
  • You can request deletion of account-associated data or ask privacy questions by emailing ify0222yfi@gmail.com (please include your in-app anonymous user/device ID if available; never email passwords or payment card numbers).
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Subscription Settings.

9. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and administrative safeguards, including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), signed API requests, and strict access controls. No system is completely secure, so please avoid submitting highly sensitive personal data unless necessary.

10. International Processing

Because our cloud infrastructure and third-party AI and payment service providers operate across multiple regions, your data may be processed outside your country or region of residence.

11. Children's Privacy

Co-work is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it appropriately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our product, service providers, or legal requirements evolve. Updates are reflected by revising the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page, and material changes will be highlighted where appropriate.

隐私政策

本隐私政策适用于 iOS、iPadOS 和 macOS 平台的 Co-work 应用（包括 App Store 版与 Mac 官网版）及相关后台服务，覆盖 AI 对话、联网搜索、网页读取与文档解析、Mac Agent 任务、助手、图像生成、AI 内容举报、反馈、积分及 Apple 自动续费订阅等功能。

2. 我们处理的信息

根据你实际使用的功能，我们可能处理以下类别的信息：

  • 匿名账号与设备标识：本机生成的设备标识、与其关联的内部匿名用户 ID 及访问鉴权令牌。
  • 账号、会员与积分状态：会员档位、积分余额、使用计数与积分账本记录。
  • 对话、搜索与文档内容：你提交的提示词、会话标题、消息历史、所选模型、助手上下文、你要求读取的网页链接、附加的图片或文档（如 PDF、Word、PPT），以及返回给你的 AI 回复。
  • 语音输入（iOS）：当你在 iOS 上使用语音转文字输入时，语音会通过 Apple 系统语音识别框架实时转写为文本，供你确认后发送。
  • Mac Agent 任务内容：你提交的任务指令、所选工作区、权限模式、工具或命令输出、本地文件路径、元数据，以及仅在你主动选择或授权 Agent 检查、总结、编辑或创建文件时的相关文件内容。本地工作区文件保留在你的 Mac 本机，不会在未经任务授权时上传。
  • 图像生成数据：生图提示词、可选参考图片、尺寸/分辨率/质量参数以及生成的图片结果。
  • 内容举报与反馈数据：举报对象标识、举报原因、选填补充说明、被举报 AI 回复的必要摘录、生成的工单编号（CR-...），以及你在反馈中提交的内容和可选联系邮箱。
  • 本机应用设置：语言偏好、所选模型、AI 数据共享授权状态、工作区配置及权限偏好等保存在设备本地的数据。
  • 订阅与购买验证记录：Apple 商品标识、交易号、原始交易号、签名交易信息或回执、环境信息、到期时间，以及用于将购买绑定至匿名账号的 app account token。我们不会获取你的完整银行卡号或支付密码。
  • 推送设备令牌（iOS）：在 iOS 正式版中，若你开启通知权限，我们会处理 Apple 推送通知服务（APNs）设备令牌，用于在后台生成回复完成后向你发送提醒。
  • 产品分析与安全风控数据：不含聊天正文的脱敏产品交互事件、会员状态、故障诊断信息，以及用于限流、请求签名校验、反欺诈与服务稳定性的 IP 地址和请求元数据。你的提示词、聊天记录和文件内容绝不会发送给产品分析工具。

我们仅在运行、保障与改进 Co-work 所需的范围内使用上述信息，包括：

  • 创建并维护匿名应用账号、会员权益与积分余额。
  • 在你同意 AI 数据共享授权后，提供 AI 对话、联网搜索、网页读取、文档解析、助手、Mac Agent 及图像生成服务。

  • 在 iOS 端开启通知权限时，推送后台回复完成提醒。

5.3 统计与分析服务

  • Google Firebase Analytics（仅 iOS 正式版）：在 iOS App Store 正式版中用于统计应用功能使用事件（如页面访问、功能交互、会员状态）及衡量产品稳定性。提示词、聊天正文、AI 回复及用户文件内容绝不会发送至 Firebase Analytics。

我们不会出售你的个人信息，也不将收集的信息用于跨公司广告追踪。

6. AI 数据共享控制与内容举报

  • 明确授权与随时撤回：首次发送 AI 请求前，Co-work 会弹窗说明发送的数据范围及第三方 AI 服务商。你可选择暂不同意而不影响查看设置或管理订阅，也可随时在 iOS（我的 → 数据共享） 或 macOS（设置 → 隐私） 中重新授权或关闭。关闭后，在重新授权前不会再向 AI 服务商发送新的提示词、图片或文件。
  • 内容举报与即时移除：每条 AI 文本回复、Agent 回复及生成图片均提供「举报」入口。提交后，该内容会立即从界面与历史记录中移除并显示工单号（CR-...），刷新或重启后保持移除状态，且不会再进入后续模型上下文。在 macOS 上，举报 Agent 回复不会修改或删除你本地工作区中的文件。

7. 数据保留与删除

我们仅在提供服务、维护订阅记录、处理内容举报、防范滥用及履行法律义务所合理需要的期限内保留数据：

  • 会话历史、生成图片记录及 Agent 任务记录会保留至你在应用内删除或申请删除账号为止。
  • 内容举报记录与反馈提交内容可能会保留一段合理期限，用于安全审核、反滥用与支持跟进。
  • 订阅核验、账务与反欺诈记录可能会按合规与财务要求继续保留。
  • 若删除匿名账号时仍有有效 Apple 订阅，为便于你在新匿名账号中恢复权益，我们仅保留原始交易号与 app account token 的不可逆 HMAC 指纹（不含原始交易号、聊天或文件内容），并在恢复后标记为已使用。
  • 安全与限流元数据仅在有限运营周期内保留。
