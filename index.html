Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

Overview

co-work is a Mac application that provides AI-powered collaboration assistants. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle information in our application.

Key Point: co-work does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data.

Information We Do NOT Collect

co-work does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • User account information (no login required)
  • Payment information (the app is free)
  • Device information or identifiers
  • Usage data or analytics
  • Location data
  • Conversation history or content
  • Any files or system resources from your device

How the App Works

co-work provides multiple AI assistants (such as Product Manager, Developer, etc.) that you can interact with through conversations. All conversations are:

  • Processed locally or through secure AI service providers
  • Isolated from each other
  • Not stored or logged by us
  • Not shared with any third parties

System Access

co-work does not access any local files, folders, or system resources on your Mac. The app operates entirely within its own sandbox environment.

Third-Party Services

co-work may use third-party AI service providers to power the AI assistants. Any data transmitted to these services is subject to their respective privacy policies. We do not control or have access to any data processed by these services.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store any user data, there is no user data at risk. All conversations are ephemeral and exist only during your active session.

Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
